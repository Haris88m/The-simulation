01 · The system

A server-authoritative universe.

The AI is an interchangeable interpreter. The authoritative server owns identity, evidence, world state, simulation, checkpoints, permissions, and history.

Any premise may be proposed and simulated.

Scientific integrity is maintained through provenance. A fantasy can exist without being mislabeled as evidence. Contradictory worlds may continue in parallel.

02 · Scientific contract

Possibility is not prediction.

The project deliberately separates unrestricted worldbuilding from testable forecasting.

Possibility track

Explore counterfactuals, metaphysics, alternate physics, political systems, technologies, and new variables. Evaluate consequences, interactions, sensitivity, robustness, and novelty.

Forecast track

Only predefined questions with deadlines, resolution rules, evidence cutoffs, and recorded probabilities. Reality later scores them; failed forecasts remain visible.

Compact kernel

Fixed-point and integer methods preserve the 1980s/1990s computing principle while modern infrastructure multiplies and persists the worlds.

Calibrated uncertainty

Monte Carlo ensembles, causal interventions, sequential evidence updates, sensitivity analysis, and proper scoring are evaluated separately.

No secret value function

“Better” is multidimensional. Players may value freedom, equality, health, prosperity, faith, security, ecology, knowledge, or other declared outcomes differently.

03 · Any intelligence

Bring your own model.

ChatGPT and Claude are examples, not dependencies. A player may use a local open-source model, a personal model, a custom multi-agent system, or no AI at all.

Structured exchange

Signed authority

An AI can misstate a result. The signed server transition, checkpoint, model version, and event chain remain canonical.
